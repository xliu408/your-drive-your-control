Who we are

About our campaign

A student-led social marketing project encouraging young drivers to keep their attention where it belongs: on the road.

Our purpose

Fewer injuries. Safer journeys.

Our public health goal is to help reduce injuries associated with texting while driving among young drivers.

The behavior we want to encourage is straightforward: don't read, check, or respond to messages while driving. Silence notifications and put the phone somewhere out of reach before starting a trip.

Many young people already know texting and driving is risky. The harder part can be changing a habit or resisting the pressure to answer a message right away. We want to make the safer choice easier.

What guides us

More than a warning sign.

Our campaign is built around what many young drivers value: the freedom and confidence that come with being able to drive.

01

Independence

Phone-free driving helps young drivers protect the freedom that comes with being behind the wheel.

02

Simple habits

We focus on actions that can fit into a regular routine instead of expecting people to change overnight.

03

Positive influence

Friends, passengers, and family can help make paying attention to the road feel normal.
