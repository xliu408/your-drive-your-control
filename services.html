Small changes you can use right away to keep your phone from becoming the most distracting thing in your car.

Before you drive

Build your own phone-free routine.

Pick the steps that work for you, and practice them before each drive. You shouldn't have to fight the urge to check every notification.

STEP 01

Turn on a driving mode

Silence incoming notifications using Driving Focus on iPhone or a driving/Do Not Disturb mode on Android.

See setup help ↓
STEP 02

Put your phone out of reach

Move it into a bag, pouch, or another spot you won't be tempted to grab while driving.

Take the pledge →
STEP 03

Plan before you go

Set navigation and music before moving, and tell people you will respond after arriving.

Phone setup

Make notifications less tempting.

Settings can look a little different depending on the phone and software version. These official guides have the latest steps.

iOS

For iPhone

  1. Open Settings and select Focus.
  2. Choose Driving and set when it turns on.
  3. Check the notification and auto-reply settings before your next trip.
Apple's Driving Focus guide ↗
A

For Android

  1. Open Settings and look for Modes or Do Not Disturb.
  2. Set up Driving mode if available on your device.
  3. Check your settings before starting the car.
Google's Android guide ↗
Friends help friends

Riding with someone? Be part of the solution.

Passengers can help with directions, handle messages, or simply remind their friend that the text can wait.

  • Offer to manage navigation or music before the car moves.
  • If your friend reaches for their phone, give a friendly reminder.
  • Don't expect immediate replies when you know someone is driving.
  • Set an example by using the same habits when it's your turn to drive.

“I'll get back to you when I'm parked.”

That one message before a trip can take the pressure off answering later.
